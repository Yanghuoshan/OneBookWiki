## Chapter Purpose

Establish attention as a deliberate reading practice.

## Executive Summary

The chapter argues that a reader should begin with a question and distinguish evidence from decoration.

## Core Thesis

Attention becomes more durable when it is organized around a question.

## Argument Map

A question directs notice; directed notice reveals evidence; evidence supports later recall.

## Key Concepts

- [Question-led reading](../concepts/question-led-reading.md)

## Evidence and Examples

The method has 3 steps: name the question, mark the evidence, and record what remains unresolved.

## Important Quotations
