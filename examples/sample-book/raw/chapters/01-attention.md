## Attention as a practice

Attention is a practice rather than a resource that can simply be spent. The reader must decide what deserves sustained notice before starting an argument.

The author writes, "attention becomes durable when it is attached to a question". A reading session that begins with a question can separate evidence from decoration.

## A small method

The method has 3 steps: name the question, mark the evidence, and record what remains unresolved.
